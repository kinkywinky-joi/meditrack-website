The problem

Missed doses quietly undermine treatment

Forgetfulness, busy routines, several medicines and complicated timings make adherence hard, particularly for people living with long-term conditions.

~50%

of patients with chronic diseases do not take their medicines as prescribed.
WHO, Adherence to Long-Term Therapies: Evidence for Action, 2003

Forgotten doses

Phone alarms get ignored, and handwritten notes are hard to keep current.

Confusing schedules

Multiple medicines with different timings make the right routine difficult to follow.

Heavy caregiver load

Family members are left to remind, and are not always available.

About

Built by pharmacy students who understand why it matters

We are a student venture from DPGU SPR, Pimpri, Pune, developed under the Wadhwani Foundation venture programme. Our goal is to use pharmacy knowledge to help people remember their medicines, follow treatment properly and live healthier.
