The problem

Missed doses quietly undermine treatment

Forgetfulness, busy routines, several medicines and complicated timings make adherence hard, particularly for people living with long-term conditions.

~50%

of patients with chronic diseases do not take their medicines as prescribed.WHO, Adherence to Long-Term Therapies: Evidence for Action, 2003

1

Forgotten doses

Phone alarms get ignored, and handwritten notes are hard to keep current.

2

Confusing schedules

Multiple medicines with different timings make the right routine difficult to follow.

3

Heavy caregiver load

Family members are left to remind, and are not always available.
